# El Patio — Design System

## World

El Patio is a sober night football club translated into a mobile booking tool. Its own-world signature is a tactical pitch: thin court lines, position dots, measured labels and clear time blocks. The pattern appears where it carries product meaning—venue placeholders, confirmation, and operational states—rather than as wallpaper.

## Color

- Ink: `#0B0E0C`
- Forest surface: `#123126`
- Grass action: `#197A49`
- Lime marker: `#B7EE55`
- Warm text: `#F4F7F2`
- Muted text: `#9DAAA0`
- Hairline: warm white at low opacity

Lime is reserved for active date, focus, availability, ratings and confirmed states. Surfaces stay dark and quiet; there are no rainbow gradients or gamer effects.

## Type and shape

Manrope is the primary sans-serif. Display copy uses tight tracking and short line lengths; prices, dates and time slots use the same face with heavier weight for scanning. Cards use 16px radius, controls use 8–10px radius and borders are always subtle. Depth is created by low-contrast surfaces and soft offset shadows only where needed.

## Components

The public surface includes pitch placeholders, search, date rail, filter chips, court cards, detail availability, sticky reservation CTA, confirmation ticket, bottom navigation, empty states and saved/bookings surfaces. Administration reuses the brand, tokens, status dots, tables and pitch-line login treatment with a denser operational layout.

## Motion and states

Interactions use restrained lift and color changes; the authored moment is the selected time slot and the confirmation transition. Every primary control has disabled, focus and selected states. Empty results, form validation, filter reset and operational empty states are explicit.
